# AI on Trial

AI on Trial is an interactive physical computing project exploring bias and automated decision-making in AI-driven recruitment.

The project uses physical interaction, sensors, visual feedback and intentionally questionable assessment criteria to demonstrate how automated systems can reduce complex human behaviour into simplified data.

Participants complete a simulated job interview while the system evaluates seemingly irrelevant physical information such as balance, movement and clothing colour. The experience uses humour and exaggeration to encourage users to question fairness, transparency and bias in automated decision-making.

## How It Works

Participants take part in a mock AI-powered job interview while standing on an interactive physical platform.

The system collects physical inputs such as movement, balance and sensor data and converts them into visual and physical feedback. These measurements are deliberately treated as if they were meaningful indicators of employability.

The final decision is presented through an intentionally arbitrary physical mechanism, reinforcing the project's critique of over-reliance on automated systems for complex human decisions.
